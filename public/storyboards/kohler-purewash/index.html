01 / Film overview

The idea behind the edit

The storyboard moves from form and component detail into the water/pressure story, then closes with the product in a complete bathroom context.

The film opens with close, sculptural product views against black, moves into water and pressure comparisons, then resolves into a complete bathroom and the words “HYGIENE · CONVENIENCE · SUSTAINABILITY.”

02 / Art direction

Colour, material & light

Porcelain whites, controlled water highlights and a clean contrast between product reveal and lived-in bathroom.

Porcelain#F8F9F7 · Clean product silhouette
Cool mist#DDE4E4 · Clinical neutral field
Waterline teal#278C94 · Flow and interface accents
Graphite#252A2D · Product-reveal contrast
Brushed chrome#B8C1C4 · Fixture highlights
Stone grey#9A9D9B · Bathroom surfaces

Light & contrast

Start in near-black for the white product to emerge cleanly; move into soft, cool bathroom daylight for context.

Materials

Porcelain, satin metal, cool stone and glass. Keep white product edges readable against either dark or mid-tone backgrounds.

Graphics

Thin white callouts, restrained cyan/teal highlights and compact numeric labels. Keep comparison panels clear and legible.
