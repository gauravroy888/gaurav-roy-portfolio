02 / Art direction

Finish, light & sculptural form

Black negative space, a warm metallic hero, precise profile highlights and editorial typography form the core visual language.

Black void#050607 · Premium negative space
Warm graphite#1D1B19 · Soft shadow field
Champagne gold#C7A36A · Hero finish / light pool
Burnished bronze#825D3E · Metallic depth
Warm ivory#F4EBDD · Text highlights
Soft amber light#E8C98E · Pedestal glow

Light & atmosphere

Keep the environment nearly black. A narrow warm key light traces the faucet’s blade-like profile while a circular floor glow gives it a stage.

Form & finish

Preserve crisp edges and controlled metal reflections. Avoid broad white reflections that flatten the sculpted silhouette.

Typography & motion

Small premium labels, short feature headings and slow fades. Feature text should never compete with the faucet; keep the hero consistently visible.
