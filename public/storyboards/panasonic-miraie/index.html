Creative premise

A guided path from app download to a connected, controllable air conditioner.

The tutorial should feel reassuring and easy to follow rather than like a high-drama advertisement. A consistent room scene anchors the walkthrough; the phone is the changing interface. Every new step title should reset the viewer’s attention, followed by one explicit action and one clear confirmation state.

Production direction

Guidance for recreating, revising or producing a related version of this advertisement.

Shape & material

The AC, phone and living room are rendered cleanly with smooth, softened geometry and a pale blue-grey wall. The mobile interface should remain the sharpest element in each tutorial frame. Use white translucent text panels with sufficient contrast, and keep labels large enough for desktop and mobile playback.

Colour & lighting

Broad, diffuse daylight through the window, soft shadows under the AC and furniture, and a low-contrast palette. Device-pairing frames can add a circular blue connection pulse; reserve stronger blue for actionable UI elements and confirmations.

Camera & motion

Use stable 3D room compositions for instructions. Animate the phone with a slow perspective tilt only in feature-showcase sections; during setup steps, keep the phone front-on. Introduce each numbered step with a clean title card and give every key instruction enough dwell time to read.

Sound direction

Gentle technology bed, quiet transition chimes between numbered steps and very soft touch confirmations. Avoid urgency. If narration is added, read the instruction in the same order as the on-screen overlay.

Optional voiceover / narration adaptation

OPTIONAL ADAPTATION (not a transcript): “Download the Panasonic MirAIe app and sign up. Verify your mobile number, then choose Add a new device. Press SMART on the AC remote until the Wi-Fi indicator blinks. Select your home Wi-Fi network and enter the password. Once setup is complete, open the dashboard to adjust your AC, create sleep profiles and access service features.”

Final review checklist

  • Check the app screens against the latest MirAIe release before publication; the tutorial contains specific OS-version copy.
  • Never expose a real mobile number, OTP, email, Wi-Fi SSID or password in demonstration footage.
  • Verify pairing instructions against the exact AC model and remote in the final instructional version.

Keep original on-screen text and interface wording faithful to the final master. Any proposed narration in this document is adaptation copy, not a transcript of the original audio.

Setup context

Supplementary information from the brand’s official website, kept separate from what is directly visible in the video.

Panasonic’s official MirAIe support page lists an IoT-enabled Panasonic AC, a stable 2.4 GHz internet connection and a registered Indian mobile number for OTP as setup considerations. The sampled tutorial overlay mentions iOS 12 and Android 6; verify current app-store compatibility and the current support instructions before reusing those version claims.
