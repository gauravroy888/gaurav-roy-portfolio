Creative premise

Modern construction meets a precisely sculpted electrical detail.

Move from the friction of traditional construction into a confident, modern electrical product reveal. The film has two worlds: large-scale architecture and MIVAN construction, then a near-black product stage that makes the MILUZ LARA switch feel precise, tactile and considered. The final movement returns the product to a lived-in home.

Production direction

Guidance for recreating, revising or producing a related version of this advertisement.

Shape & material

Keep the switch plate square, flush and sharply legible, with soft rocker contours visible through rim lighting. Prioritize the video’s anthracite / matte-black look, controlled specular edges and uncluttered wall installation. Avoid bright glossy reflections that would hide the product’s dark geometry.

Colour & lighting

Use near-black negative space, a narrow cool rim on the product edge and faint top-side highlights. Construction scenes can be brighter and slightly desaturated; the home scenes shift toward soft daylight and warm practicals. The final brand green is a deliberate high-energy contrast.

Camera & motion

Architecture section: slow aerial drift and measured parallax. Product section: controlled 3D orbit, macro push-in and match cuts from glowing interfaces to the rocker. Lifestyle section: stable wide frames and restrained hand/phone interaction.

Sound direction

Minimal electronic pulse that starts cool, adds a soft activation cue at the futuristic hand, then warms for home/lifestyle. Keep tactile whooshes short; do not add loud button clicks unless aligned to an actual interaction.

A fusion of beauty and functionality, with a form that feels at home in the spaces we live in. Schneider Electric. Life Is On.” Use only claims approved for the market and product variant.
