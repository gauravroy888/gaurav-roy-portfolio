The idea behind the edit

The film introduces the app, opens into modules, then follows visible attendance, lead, quote, training and performance screens before the brand end card.

A tall phone sits against a soft meadow background while screens and feature tiles demonstrate a connected business workflow. The green bokeh remains consistent while the interface supplies the information hierarchy.

02 / Art direction

Interface, palette & motion

A steady meadow-green background gives the mobile interface a friendly setting, while clean white screens and KOHLER navy keep the information legible.

Meadow green#7E9B4E · Lifestyle background
Leaf light#CFDEA9 · Soft natural highlights
Sage#B8CDA1 · Secondary surface
KOHLER navy#243F7A · App navigation and panels
Dashboard blue#4A82C4 · Charts and data states
Paper white#FCFCF8 · UI legibility

Backdrop & light

Keep the out-of-focus meadow soft, bright and calm. The phone screen remains the sharpest element, with no distracting camera shake.

Interface language

Use clean white app cards, deep navy headers and blue chart blocks. Maintain readable mobile scale and consistent spacing between screen captures.

Motion & overlays

Feature tiles may float in beside the phone, but only when they correspond to a screen or module actually shown. Keep transitions smooth and instructional.

05 / Production checks

Before the next cut

Checks specific to the footage and its visible product/UI content.

Protect visible business information

The footage includes customer/lead records, quote details, a quote ID, user labels and target/performance figures. Confirm these are approved demo data or replace/redact before public distribution.

Use the actual workflow order

Preserve the film’s sequence: opening/app dashboard, modules, attendance, lead management, quote/discount screens, training, audits/tasks and target/performance views.

Avoid adding unsupported functions

Describe only modules visible in the recording. Do not infer integrations, permissions or automation behavior from a screen mock-up alone.

UI readability

Keep screen copy secondary to the phone. When creating a shortened version, zoom into each app screen rather than shrinking the whole phone until labels become unreadable.
