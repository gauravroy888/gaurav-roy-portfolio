Color & material mood board

A visual system derived from the product film’s own palette and imagery.

Stainless silver#A8B0B5Brushed metal and edge highlights

Porcelain#FFFFFFHigh-key negative space
Soft grey#D7DBDEProduct stage and dimension graphics

Brushed stainless steel

Fine linear grain, crisp edge reflections, controlled highlights; avoid mirror-like chrome if the source shows a satin finish.

Pale grey studio field

Soft neutral gradient and light contact shadow; keep the rail readable against the background.

KOHLER black mark

Small, high-contrast brand signature anchored consistently in the top-right area.

Warranty green

Use as a single meaningful accent for the two-year emblem, not as a decorative wash.

Art direction

Practical guidance for recreating the visual language.

Composition

Keep the full rack, end brackets and measurement callouts visible. Favor centered product renders and straight, uncluttered views.

Lighting

Use a broad soft key, narrow rim highlights on rails, and subtle contact shadows. Preserve separation between silver rails and grey background.

Motion

Slow turntable rotations, controlled push-ins, and clean transitions between standalone product, storage arrangement and installed context.

Graphics

Use compact all-caps section headers, thin measurement lines and restrained green marks. Keep proof points aligned and legible.

The rack emerges

On-screen text / UI

KOHLER branding and product header begin to appear.

Camera & edit

Fade up from black; keep the product floating cleanly against the empty background. Use a slow, precise 3D reveal.

Script / sound

A restrained premium cue; let the reveal breathe without a heavy impact.

Shot 0200:02 – 00:04

Superior craftsmanship

The camera moves closer to the rack, emphasizing straight rails, squared brackets and the satin metallic finish.

On-screen text / UI

“SUPERIOR CRAFTSMANSHIP” · “Brushed Stainless steel finish from a smooth surface” (verify capitalization against master).

Camera & edit

Controlled 3/4 orbit with a bright, soft edge highlight that separates steel from grey.

Script / sound

Light metallic shimmer or airy tonal lift; no literal tool sounds needed.

Shot 0300:04 – 00:10.5

Finish and longevity

On-screen text / UI

Green “2 YEAR” emblem; product/finish header remains on screen.

Camera & edit

Use slow turntable movement and measured cuts. Keep typography and emblem within a stable safe area.

Script / sound

Maintain a quiet premium instrumental bed; small tonal accent when the emblem lands.

Multipurpose storage

“MULTIPURPOSE” · “Convenient space and hanging for towels when in use”.

Transition from a single product render to the functional arrangement; show the full width and both storage levels.

Script / sound

Add a subtle lift as towels and accessories populate the rack.

Shot 0500:17.3 – 00:21.5

Built for everyday use

A loaded rack and graphic weight readout introduce a durability proof point.

On-screen text / UI

“DURABLE & LONG LASTING” · “High weight bearing capacity of 20 KG”; numeric display appears as “02300”/counter style in the sampled frame—verify the exact motion graphic in the master.

Camera & edit

Hold the complete rack in view while the weight graphic resolves. Avoid cropping the end brackets.

Script / sound

Soft mechanical click as the number settles; keep it tasteful.

Shot 0600:21.5 – 00:24.3

Easy to install

A clean wall view introduces the mounting location, followed by the rack fixed in position.

On-screen text / UI

“EASY TO INSTALL” · “Comes with mounting kit for installation”.

Camera & edit

Use a straight-on installation view, then a slight angle to communicate depth and wall clearance.

Script / sound

A simple confirmation tick can punctuate the fit; music remains understated.

Shot 0700:24.3 – 00:27.2

Compact proportions
